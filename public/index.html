Know your trainer

With over 20 years of hands-on experience, Md Shahadat Hussain, Founder and Lead Instructor at Raniganj Dynamic Technical Institute, brings practical knowledge of AC and refrigeration, electrical work, and welding into every lesson. Trained in Bengal, he believes that the strongest technical skills are built through experience, patience, and learning by doing.

At Raniganj Dynamic Technical Institute, this belief shapes every step of the learning journey. Students learn by working with real equipment, developing practical skills under the guidance of an experienced instructor who understands the value of hands-on training. The goal is simple: to help every student build the confidence, competence, and job-ready skills needed to turn technical knowledge into a career.
